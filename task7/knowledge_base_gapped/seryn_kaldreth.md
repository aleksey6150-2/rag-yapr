# Seryn Kaldreth

## Overview
Princess Seryn Kaldreth is a human female who served as a member of the Dominion Assembly, a leader of the Free Coalition, and later a general of the New Stellar Republic. She is the daughter of Ankarin Venarix and Pyrenne Amalis.

## Early Life
Adopted at birth by Bailen Kaldreth of Vareth, Seryn grew up as a princess and was raised to believe Bailen and Brea Kaldreth were her biological parents. Her twin brother Kael Venarix was raised separately on Seldaris.

## Role in the Coalition
Seryn was instrumental in the Free Coalition's efforts against the Iron Dominion. She was captured aboard the Voyager IV while carrying stolen Void Core plans and later rescued by Kael Venarix and Drexan Morr.

## Relationships
- Biological parents: Ankarin Venarix, Pyrenne Amalis
- Twin brother: Kael Venarix
- Husband: Drexan Morr
- Adoptive father: Bailen Kaldreth

## Synth Flux Abilities
Seryn inherited strong Flux sensitivity from her father, though she trained only briefly as a Veylar.
