# Talon-class fighter

## Overview
The Talon-class fighter, officially the T-65 Talon, is a single-pilot starfighter produced by Incom Corporation. It became the signature fighter of the Free Coalition during the Dominion War.

## Design
The fighter is named for its wings, which split into an X-shape during combat to allow wider firing angles and better heat dissipation. Each wingtip mounts a laser cannon.

## Armament
Talon starfighters are equipped with four laser cannons and two nova torpedo launchers. They also feature a warp coil, allowing for independent travel between star systems.

## Astromech
Each Talon carries an astromech droid that assists with navigation, repairs, and system monitoring. Kael Venarix's astromech was Z7-K2.

## Notable Engagements
Talon starfighters played central roles in the Clash of Kareth (destroying the first Void Core), the Clash of Kyrnal evacuation, and the Clash of Morvenn. They became symbols of the Free Coalition's resistance.
