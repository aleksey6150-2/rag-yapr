# Kyrnal

## Overview
Kyrnal is a remote ice planet in the Outer Sectors, covered entirely by snow and ice. Its frigid temperatures make it nearly uninhabitable.

## Climate
Temperatures on Kyrnal fall far below freezing. Massive blizzards and ice storms sweep across the planet's surface, and nighttime temperatures are lethal to most species without protective gear.

## Wildlife
Native creatures include the tauntaun, a reptomammal used as a mount by the Free Coalition, and the wampa, a large predator that hunts in the ice caves.

## Coalition Base
The Free Coalition established Echo Base on Kyrnal, concealed within massive ice caverns. The location was chosen for its isolation and the planet's low value as a strategic target.

## Clash of Kyrnal
The Iron Dominion discovered Echo Base and launched a ground assault using Colossus walkers. The Free Coalition was forced to evacuate, though they successfully delayed the Dominion advance long enough for most of their forces to escape.
