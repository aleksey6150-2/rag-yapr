# Grommak

## Overview
Grommak is a male Grommakin from the planet Kashir. He serves as co-pilot of the Starfang and is the lifelong companion of Drexan Morr.

## Background
Grommak fought in the Shard Wars as a warrior of Kashir and was later enslaved by the Iron Dominion. He was freed by Drexan Morr, who was at the time an Dominion officer. Grommak pledged a life debt to Drexan from that moment on.

## Abilities
Grommak is physically powerful, standing over two meters tall, and is highly skilled as a pilot, mechanic, and warrior. He is fluent in several languages but speaks primarily in the Grommakin language Grommakese.

## Weapons
His signature weapon is a pulse-bow, a traditional Grommakin ranged weapon.

## Role in the Coalition
Grommak fought alongside the Free Coalition in numerous operations, including the Clash of Morvenn, where he helped disable the shield generator protecting the second Void Core.
