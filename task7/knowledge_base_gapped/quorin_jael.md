# Quorin Jael

## Overview
Quorin Jael was a human male Veylar Elder known for his independent thinking and strong connection to the Living Synth Flux. He was the master of Velran Tessik.

## Philosophy
Quorin often disagreed with the Veylarian Conclave, favoring intuition and present-focused Flux sensitivity over strict doctrine. He was never elevated to the Council despite his skill.

## Discovery of Ankarin
On a mission to Seldaris, Quorin discovered a young slave named Ankarin Venarix with an unprecedented connection to the Synth Flux. He believed Ankarin was the Chosen One prophesied to bring balance to the Synth Flux.

## Combat
Quorin wielded a green-bladed plasma-blade and was a skilled practitioner of plasma-blade Form IV.

## Death
Quorin was killed on Verenthia by the Dorveth apprentice Xarn Scourge during the Clash of Verenthia. With his dying words, he asked Velran Tessik to train Ankarin Venarix. He later became one of the first Veylar to retain consciousness after death through the Synth Flux.
