# Morvenn

## Overview
Morvenn is a forest moon in the Outer Sectors, covered in vast ancient woodlands. It is home to the Ewoks, a species of small, furry bipeds.

## Environment
The moon features towering trees, dense forest canopies, rivers, and rocky outcrops. The Ewoks build their villages high in the trees, connected by wooden walkways.

## Ewoks
The Ewoks are a primitive species skilled in woodcraft, trapping, and stone-age combat. Despite their low technology, they are brave warriors who fiercely defend their home.

## Dominion Presence
The Iron Dominion built a shield generator on Morvenn's surface to protect the second Void Core in orbit.

## Clash of Morvenn
The Free Coalition launched a combined operation on Morvenn: a ground team led by Drexan Morr attacked the shield generator with Morveni support, while Coalition fleet ships engaged the Dominion Fleet in orbit. The resulting victory destroyed the second Void Core and killed Overlord Nocturn.
