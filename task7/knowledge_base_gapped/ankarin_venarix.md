# Ankarin Venarix

## Overview
Ankarin Venarix was a human male born on Seldaris to Shema Venarix. Discovered by Veylar Elder Quorin Jael, he was trained in the Veylarian Order due to his extraordinary connection to the Synth Flux.

## Early Life
Ankarin was a slave on Seldaris before being freed and brought to the Veylarian Temple on Pyrentis. Quorin believed Ankarin was the Chosen One prophesied to bring balance to the Synth Flux.

## Training
He was apprenticed to Velran Tessik and became a skilled pilot, swordsman, and mechanic. During the Shard Wars, he served as a Veylar Sentinel and general of the Grand Legion of the Stellar Republic.

## Relationships
- Mentor: Quorin Jael (briefly), then Velran Tessik
- Wife: Pyrenne Amalis (secret marriage)
- Children: Kael Venarix and Seryn Kaldreth

## Fall
Manipulated by Prime Arbiter Nocturn and fearing the death of his wife, Ankarin fell to the Dorveth Current of the Synth Flux and became Xarn Velgor.
