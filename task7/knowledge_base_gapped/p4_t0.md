# P4-T0

## Overview
P4-T0 is a protocol droid fluent in over six million forms of communication. He was built by Ankarin Venarix on Seldaris as a young boy and later served the Venarix family across multiple generations.

## Purpose
Protocol droids are designed for etiquette, translation, and diplomatic functions. P4-T0 frequently served as an interpreter during tense negotiations.

## Appearance
P4-T0 has a golden humanoid chassis, though one of his legs was silver for a period. He is notably anxious in personality, often worrying about odds of survival in dangerous situations.

## Service History
P4-T0 served Pyrenne Amalis, Bailen Kaldreth on Vareth, and later Princess Seryn Kaldreth and the Free Coalition. He is constantly accompanied by Z7-K2.

## Role in Key Events
On Morvenn, the Ewoks mistook P4-T0 for a deity due to his golden appearance, which helped the Free Coalition secure their cooperation during the Clash of Morvenn.
