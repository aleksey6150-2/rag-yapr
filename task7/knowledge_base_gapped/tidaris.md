# Tidaris

## Overview
Tidaris is a remote ocean planet in the Outer Sectors, covered entirely by water. Its cities are built on stilts rising above the storm-swept seas.

## Inhabitants
The Tidarans are a tall, slender species with long necks and elongated faces. They are known for their advanced cloning technology and their careful, reserved demeanor.

## Cloning Facilities
Tidaris is the stellar center for cloning technology. The Tidarans produced the Echelon Host used by the Stellar Republic during the Shard Wars, using the bounty hunter Jorran Korr as the genetic template.

## Climate
Tidaris experiences constant heavy rain and storms, with massive waves crashing against the supports of its cities. The weather is rarely calm.

## Strategic Importance
The discovery of Tidaris and its echelon host triggered the outbreak of the Shard Wars. After the rise of the Dominion, Tidaris's cloning facilities declined in importance as the Dominion transitioned to recruited stormtroopers.
