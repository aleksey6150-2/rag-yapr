# Z7-K2

## Overview
Z7-K2 is an astromech droid of the R2 series, manufactured by Industrial Automaton. Despite his small size, he played a central role in many of the Expanse's most pivotal events.

## Service History
Z7-K2 served Queen Pyrenne Amalis of Verenthia, then Ankarin Venarix and Velran Tessik during the Shard Wars. After the fall of the Republic, he accompanied Bailen Kaldreth and eventually came into the service of Kael Venarix.

## Design
Standing about one meter tall, Z7-K2 is equipped with numerous tools including a fusion welder, computer interface arm, holographic projector, and astrogation computer. He communicates through beeps and whistles.

## Notable Deeds
Z7-K2 carried the Void Core plans that were recovered by the Free Coalition, delivered Princess Seryn's distress message to Velran Tessik, and provided critical support during the Clash of Kareth aboard Kael Venarix's Talon.

## Companion
Z7-K2 is frequently paired with the protocol droid P4-T0.
