# Verenthia

## Overview
Verenthia is a lush, green planet in the Mid Sectors, known for its rolling hills, plains, lakes, and classical architecture. It is the homeworld of Pyrenne Amalis and Overlord Nocturn.

## Inhabitants
Verenthia is home to two intelligent species: the human Verenthia, who live on the surface in cities like Theralis, and the Gungans, an amphibious species who live in underwater cities.

## Government
Verenthia's human population is led by an elected monarch, typically a young person chosen for wisdom and character. Notable queens include Pyrenne Amalis.

## History
The Commerce Consortium invaded Verenthia, blockading and occupying the planet. The crisis was resolved with the help of the Veylar and the Aquani Grand Army, leading to the Clash of Verenthia.

## Culture
Verenthia is known for its art, architecture, and peaceful lifestyle. Theralis, its capital, features grand palaces, plazas, and waterfalls.
