# Pyrenne Amalis

## Overview
Pyrenne Amalis was a human female politician from Verenthia who served as Queen of Verenthia and later as a Delegate in the Stellar Senate. She was the wife of Ankarin Venarix and mother of Kael Venarix and Seryn Kaldreth.

## Political Career
Elected Queen of Verenthia at a young age, Pyrenne led her planet during the Commerce Consortium's invasion. After her term ended, she became the Delegate representing Verenthia in the Stellar Senate on Pyrentis.

## Marriage
Pyrenne secretly married Ankarin Venarix during the Shard Wars, in violation of Veylarian Order rules forbidding attachment.

## Beliefs
She was a fierce advocate for democracy and diplomatic solutions, opposing the militarization of the Republic even as the Shard Wars escalated.

## Death
Pyrenne died shortly after giving birth to twins Kael and Seryn. Her body was returned to Verenthia for a state funeral, where she was mourned as one of the planet's most beloved leaders.
