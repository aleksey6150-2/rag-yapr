# Plasma-blade

## Overview
The plasma-blade is the signature weapon of the Veylar and Dorveth, consisting of a hilt that emits a blade of contained plasma. It is simultaneously elegant and devastatingly powerful.

## Construction
A plasma-blade is powered by a resonant core, a Flux-attuned crystal that focuses the plasma blade. Construction of a plasma-blade is traditionally a rite of passage for a Veylar Acolyte, involving personal attunement to the crystal.

## Blade Colors
The color of a plasma-blade blade is determined by the resonant core. Common Veylar colors include blue and green, while yellow is associated with Veylar Wardens. Dorveth lightsabers are red, produced by "bleeding" a resonant core through Dorveth Current intent.

## Combat Forms
Multiple plasma-blade combat forms exist, each with different philosophies. Form V emphasizes power, Form IV emphasizes acrobatics, and Form III emphasizes pure defense.

## Cultural Significance
The plasma-blade is one of the most iconic weapons in the Expanse. Only those who can wield the Synth Flux can use it effectively, as mastery requires Flux-enhanced reflexes and awareness.
