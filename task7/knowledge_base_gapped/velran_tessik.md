# Velran Tessik

## Overview
Velran Tessik was a human male Veylar Elder who played a central role in the Shard Wars and the early Dominion War. He trained Ankarin Venarix and later Kael Venarix.

## Training
Apprenticed to Quorin Jael, Velran was knighted after his master's death on Verenthia and took on Ankarin Venarix as his own Acolyte.

## Shard Wars
During the Shard Wars, Velran served as a Veylar general, known for his skill in plasma-blade combat and diplomatic negotiations. He was a member of the Veylarian Conclave.

## Exile
After the fall of the Veylarian Order and his duel with the fallen Ankarin on Pyrovex, Velran went into hiding on Seldaris, watching over Kael Venarix from a distance.

## Final Role
Velran revealed the truth about the Synth Flux to Kael and guided him to Ozul for further training. He died during a duel with Xarn Velgor aboard the Void Core but continued to guide Kael as a Flux echo.
