# Pyrentis

## Overview
Pyrentis is a planet-wide city located in the stellar core, serving as the capital of the Stellar Republic and later the Iron Dominion. Its entire surface is covered by a massive urban sprawl of buildings, skyscrapers, and transport systems.

## Government
Pyrentis hosted the Stellar Senate and the office of the Prime Arbiter. After the fall of the Republic, it became the seat of the Iron Dominion.

## Veylarian Temple
The Veylarian Temple on Pyrentis was the headquarters of the Veylarian Order. It housed the Veylarian Conclave chambers, training halls, and a vast library of knowledge.

## Levels
Pyrentis's city extends downward through thousands of levels. The upper levels are wealthy and well-lit, while the lower levels are dangerous, poorly regulated zones filled with crime and poverty.

## Population
Home to over a trillion inhabitants of many species, Pyrentis is one of the most populous planets in the Expanse.
