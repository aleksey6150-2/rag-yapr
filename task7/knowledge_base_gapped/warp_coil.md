# Warp Coil

## Overview
The warp coil is a faster-than-light propulsion technology that allows starships to travel between star systems in reasonable timeframes. It is the foundation of stellar civilization, trade, and warfare.

## Function
The warp coil propels a ship into warpspace, an alternate dimension where travel between widely separated points becomes possible. Ships follow established warp routes that have been mapped and verified as safe.

## Classes
Hyperdrives are rated by class, with lower numbers indicating faster drives. A Class 1.0 warp coil is military-grade, while civilian ships typically have Class 2.0 or slower drives. The Starfang is famously equipped with a Class 0.5 warp coil.

## Navigation
Hyperspace navigation requires precise calculations to avoid mass shadows, which can pull ships out of warpspace and destroy them. Astromech droids typically handle these calculations aboard small starfighters.

## Limitations
A warp coil cannot be used within a planet's gravity well. Ships must first travel to a safe distance from planetary masses before engaging the warp coil. Some Dominion ships are equipped with interdictor technology that can artificially pull enemy ships out of warpspace.
