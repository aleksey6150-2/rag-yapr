# Shrike fighter

## Overview
The Shrike fighter is the standard starfighter of the Iron Dominion, produced in massive numbers and deployed from Star Destroyers and other Dominion vessels. Its name comes from its twin ion engines.

## Design
The Shrike fighter features a spherical cockpit flanked by two hexagonal solar collection panels. It has no warp coil and minimal life support, relying on its carrier ship for deep-space operations.

## Tactics
Shrike fighters rely on speed, agility, and overwhelming numbers rather than individual durability. They are unshielded and lightly armored, making them vulnerable but cheap to produce and easy to replace.

## Variants
Variants include the Shrike interceptor (faster and more maneuverable), the Shrike bomber (equipped for heavy ordnance), and Shrike Advanced x1 (flown by Xarn Velgor), which has a warp coil and improved shielding.

## Pilots
Shrike fighter pilots are typically trained at Dominion academies and deployed in squadrons. Their survival rate is notoriously low due to the fighter's fragility.
