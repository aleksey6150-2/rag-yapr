# Iron Dominion

## Overview
The Iron Dominion was an authoritarian stellar government established by Overlord Nocturn at the end of the Shard Wars. It replaced the Stellar Republic and ruled the Expanse for over two decades.

## Rise
The Dominion was declared by Prime Arbiter Nocturn after the elimination of the Veylarian Order through Directive Null. Nocturn cited the threat of Veylar treason and the need for a stronger, more centralized government.

## Government
The Dominion was ruled by the Emperor with absolute authority. The Dominion Assembly remained briefly as a facade of representation but was dissolved around the time of the Clash of Kareth. Regional Governors, or Prefects, administered sectors of the Expanse.

## Military
The Dominion military included the Dominion Fleet (Star Destroyers and Shrike fighters), Dominion Legion (stormtroopers and walkers), and specialized units. At its peak, the Dominion deployed over twenty-five thousand Star Destroyers.

## Fall
The Dominion collapsed after the Clash of Morvenn, in which Overlord Nocturn and Xarn Velgor were killed and the second Void Core was destroyed. Remnant Dominion forces fought on for several years before formal surrender.
