# Varran Korr

## Overview
Varran Korr is an unaltered genetic clone of the bounty hunter Jorran Korr, raised by his father as a son rather than as a soldier. He became one of the Expanse's most feared and respected bounty hunters.

## Origin
Created on Tidaris as part of the echelon host program, Varran witnessed his father's death at the hands of Veylar Elder Tessar Vaal during the Clash of Geryos.

## Equipment
Varran wears distinctive Valkari-style armor inherited from his father, including a jetpack, wrist-mounted weapons, and a T-visor helmet. He pilots a ship called Shadow I.

## Employment
Varran Korr was hired by Xarn Velgor and the Iron Dominion multiple times. He was instrumental in capturing Drexan Morr, who had been frozen in cryolite, and delivering him to Jabran the Druss on Seldaris.

## Reputation
Varran Korr is considered one of the most skilled bounty hunters in the Expanse, known for his efficiency, silence, and relentless pursuit of targets.
