# Tessar Vaal

## Overview
Tessar Vaal was a human male Veylar Elder who served as a senior member of the Veylarian Conclave during the final years of the Stellar Republic. He was known for his purple-bladed plasma-blade, a rare color in the Veylarian Order.

## Role in the Council
Second only to Ozul in authority on the Veylarian Conclave, Tessar Vaal was a respected voice in Veylar policy and strategy during the Shard Wars.

## Combat Skills
Tessar Vaal created and mastered the plasma-blade combat form known as Vaapad, which drew on the user's inner darkness while maintaining Veylar discipline. He was considered one of the greatest duelists in the Veylarian Order.

## Shard Wars
He served as a Veylar general and led the assault on Geryos that triggered the Shard Wars. He killed the bounty hunter Jorran Korr during that battle.

## Final Duel
Tessar Vaal confronted Prime Arbiter Nocturn, revealing him as the Dorveth Sovereign Xarn Nocturn. During the duel, Ankarin Venarix intervened on Nocturn's side, leading to Tessar Vaal's death.
