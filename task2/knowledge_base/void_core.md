# Void Core

## Overview
The Void Core is a moon-sized battle station built by the Iron Dominion, designed as the ultimate weapon of intimidation and power projection. Two versions of the Void Core were constructed during the Dominion War.

## Weapon
The Void Core's primary weapon is a superlaser capable of destroying an entire planet with a single shot. The weapon is powered by multiple focusing resonant cores and requires significant recharge time between shots.

## Defenses
The station is protected by thousands of pulse-cannon batteries, ion cannons, Shrike fighters, and a planetary shield. Its hull is kilometers thick.

## First Void Core
Used to destroy Vareth as a demonstration of power, the first Void Core was ultimately destroyed at the Clash of Kareth when Kael Venarix fired proton torpedoes into a thermal exhaust port, triggering a chain reaction.

## Second Void Core
A larger and more powerful version, the second Void Core was destroyed at the Clash of Morvenn when the Free Coalition disabled its shield generator and fighters penetrated to its reactor core.
