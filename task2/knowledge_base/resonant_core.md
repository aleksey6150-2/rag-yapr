# Resonant Core

## Overview
A resonant core is a rare, Flux-attuned crystal used primarily to power lightsabers. Resonant Cores also serve as focusing elements for much larger weapons, including the superlaser of the Void Core.

## Origin
Resonant Cores are found on various planets across the Expanse, most notably Iluven and later Jedaris. They grow naturally, attuned to the Synth Flux from the moment of formation.

## Personal Attunement
Resonant Cores are colorless until bonded to a Flux user. Upon bonding, the crystal takes on a color that reflects the user and their relationship to the Synth Flux. Veylar crystals typically appear blue or green; yellow occurs among Veylar Wardens.

## Corruption
A Dorveth produces a red crystal through a process called "bleeding," in which the user's Dorveth Current intent corrupts the crystal's natural resonance. This process is painful for the crystal and requires deep focus on hatred and anger.

## Military Use
The Iron Dominion harvested resonant cores in massive quantities to power the Void Core's superlaser. This required the enslavement of mining populations on Jedaris and elsewhere.
