# Thulkhar

## Overview
Thulkhar is a remote swamp planet in the Outer Sectors, covered in bogs, mist, dense jungle, and twisted trees. The planet is rarely visited and has no settlements.

## Environment
Thulkhar's surface is a perpetual mix of fog, humidity, and murky water. Visibility is low, and the local wildlife includes creatures adapted to the swamp ecosystem.

## Synth Flux Properties
Thulkhar is strong in the Synth Flux, with a Dorveth Current cave that tests those who enter it. The cave reveals truths about the visitor through visions.

## Ozul's Exile
After the fall of the Veylarian Order, Ozul went into hiding on Thulkhar, where he lived in a small hut built from local materials. He lived alone on the planet for over two decades.

## Kael's Training
Kael Venarix traveled to Thulkhar to train under Ozul in the ways of the Synth Flux. His training included physical exercises, meditation, and confronting his own fears in the Dorveth Current cave.
