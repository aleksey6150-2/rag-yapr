# Ozul

## Overview
Ozul was a legendary Veylar Elder and High Elder of the Veylarian Order. A member of a mysterious species, he lived for approximately 900 years and trained Veylar for centuries, including Kael Venarix and Count Dravek.

## Appearance
Ozul was short in stature, with green skin, long ears, and white hair in his later years. Despite his small size, he was one of the most powerful Flux users ever known.

## Role in the Order
As High Elder, Ozul led the Veylarian Conclave on Pyrentis and served as a general during the Shard Wars.

## Exile
After Directive Null and the rise of the Iron Dominion, Ozul went into exile on the swamp planet Thulkhar, where he lived in seclusion until Kael Venarix arrived for training.

## Training Kael
Ozul trained Kael Venarix in the ways of the Synth Flux on Thulkhar, teaching him Veylar philosophy and combat techniques until his death at approximately 900 years old.
