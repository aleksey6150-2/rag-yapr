# Starfang

## Overview
The Starfang is a heavily modified YT-1300 light freighter, one of the most famous starships of the Dominion War era. It is captained by Drexan Morr with Grommak as co-pilot.

## Modifications
Though the YT-1300 model is commercially unremarkable, the Starfang has been extensively modified with a military-grade warp coil, upgraded weapons, reinforced hull plating, and smuggling compartments.

## Speed
The ship is famed for its speed. Drexan Morr claims the Falcon made the Kessir Run in less than twelve parsecs, using a dangerous shortcut through hazardous space.

## History
The ship was originally owned by Landar Calystran, who lost it to Drexan Morr in a game of Vortex deck. The Falcon has changed hands several times but always returns to Drexan.

## Notable Battles
The Starfang participated in the Clash of Kareth, the escape from Kyrnal, and the Clash of Morvenn, where Landar Calystran piloted it to destroy the second Void Core's reactor core.
