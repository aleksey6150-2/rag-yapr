# Dorveth Pact

## Overview
The Dorveth Pact is an ancient organization of Flux users dedicated to the Dorveth Current. For millennia, the Dorveth were enemies of the Veylar, driven by pursuit of power, passion, and domination.

## Rule of Two
For the thousand years preceding the Dominion War, the Dorveth followed the Rule of Two: at any time, there would exist only one master and one apprentice. This rule was established to prevent the infighting that had nearly destroyed the Dorveth in ancient times.

## Philosophy
The Dorveth embrace passion, strength, power, and victory. Where the Veylar seek peace through emotional discipline, the Dorveth seek strength through emotional intensity, particularly anger, hatred, and fear.

## Notable Dorveth
During the fall of the Republic, Xarn Nocturn (Overlord Nocturn) served as master, with apprentices including Xarn Scourge, Count Dravek, and finally Xarn Velgor.

## Weapons
Dorveth wielded red-bladed lightsabers, produced by corrupting a resonant core through the Dorveth Current. Their use of the Synth Flux included abilities like Flux lightning and Flux choke.
