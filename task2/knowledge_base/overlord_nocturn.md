# Overlord Nocturn

## Overview
Overlord Nocturn, also known as Xarn Nocturn, was the Dorveth Sovereign who orchestrated the fall of the Veylarian Order, the destruction of the Stellar Republic, and the rise of the Iron Dominion. He ruled as Emperor for over two decades.

## Rise to Power
Originally a senator from Verenthia, Nocturn manipulated stellar politics to become Prime Arbiter of the Republic. He secretly controlled both sides of the Shard Wars as the Dorveth mastermind Xarn Nocturn.

## The Dominion
As Emperor, Nocturn dissolved the Senate, expanded the Dominion military, and oversaw the construction of the Void Core. He ruled through fear and the loyalty of Xarn Velgor, his apprentice.

## Synth Flux Abilities
Nocturn was a master of the Dorveth Current of the Synth Flux, capable of generating Flux lightning and deep manipulation through the Synth Flux. He wielded a red-bladed plasma-blade in combat.

## Death
Nocturn was killed aboard the second Void Core when Xarn Velgor, turning back to the Luminar Current, threw him into the station's reactor shaft.
