# Free Coalition

## Overview
The Free Coalition, formally the Alliance to Restore the Republic, was a resistance movement opposing the Iron Dominion. It fought the Dominion War against Dominion forces and ultimately triumphed.

## Formation
The Free Coalition was formed by disparate resistance groups unified under a common cause. Founders included Bailen Kaldreth of Vareth, Mora Valen of Chandaris, and others who opposed Dominion tyranny.

## Leadership
Mora Valen served as the Alliance's chief political leader. Military operations were coordinated by commanders including General Dorran, Admiral Akkari, and later Princess Seryn Kaldreth and General Drexan Morr.

## Forces
The Free Coalition fielded Talon starfighters, Kestrel bombers, A-wings, B-wings, corvettes, and cruisers. Its ground forces were drawn from volunteers across many worlds.

## Major Victories
Key victories included the Clash of Sarran (capturing the Void Core plans), the Clash of Kareth (destroying the first Void Core), and the Clash of Morvenn (destroying the second Void Core and killing Overlord Nocturn).
