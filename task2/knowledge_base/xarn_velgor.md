# Xarn Velgor

## Overview
Xarn Velgor is a Dorveth Sovereign who served as the enforcer of Overlord Nocturn and a key figure of the Iron Dominion. He was originally Ankarin Venarix, a Veylar Sentinel who fell to the Dorveth Current of the Synth Flux.

## Appearance
Velgor wears a full black life-support suit and helmet after being severely injured on Pyrovex. His breathing apparatus is one of his most recognizable traits. He wields a red-bladed plasma-blade.

## Role
As the Emperor's apprentice, Xarn Velgor hunted surviving Veylar and led Dominion military operations. He commanded Star Destroyers and oversaw the construction of the Void Core.

## Family
- Wife: Pyrenne Amalis (deceased)
- Son: Kael Venarix
- Daughter: Seryn Kaldreth
- Former Master: Velran Tessik

## Fate
Xarn Velgor eventually turned back to the Luminar Current of the Synth Flux to save his son Kael from the Emperor, killing Nocturn and restoring balance to the Synth Flux at the cost of his own life.
