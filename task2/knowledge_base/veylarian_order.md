# Veylarian Order

## Overview
The Veylarian Order is an ancient monastic and peacekeeping organization of Flux users dedicated to the Luminar Current of the Synth Flux. The Veylar served the Stellar Republic as guardians of peace and justice for thousands of years.

## Structure
The Order was led by the Veylarian Conclave, composed of twelve Veylar Masters, with Ozul serving as High Elder. The headquarters was the Veylarian Temple on Pyrentis.

## Training
Prospective Veylar were identified as young children through their Flux sensitivity. They progressed through ranks of Youngling, Acolyte, Veylar Sentinel, and Veylar Elder.

## Code
The Veylarian Code emphasized peace, serenity, harmony, and service. Veylar were forbidden from forming attachments, including marriage, and were expected to control their emotions to avoid the Dorveth Current.

## Fall of the Veylar
At the end of the Shard Wars, the Veylarian Order was nearly destroyed by Directive Null, an Dominion directive that caused the clone troopers to turn on their Veylar generals. Only a handful of Veylar survived, including Ozul and Velran Tessik.
