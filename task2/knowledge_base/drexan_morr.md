# Drexan Morr

## Overview
Drexan Morr is a human male smuggler and pilot, captain of the Starfang. He originally operated as an independent freighter pilot but joined the Free Coalition during the Dominion War.

## Background
Drexan grew up on the streets of Korelian. He served briefly in the Dominion Fleet before deserting to save a Grommakin named Grommak, who became his lifelong co-pilot and best friend.

## Ship
The Starfang is a heavily modified YT-1300 light freighter, famous for its speed. Drexan won the ship from Landar Calystran in a card game.

## Role in the Coalition
Hired by Velran Tessik and Kael Venarix to transport them to Vareth, Drexan became a reluctant hero and key figure in the Free Coalition, participating in the Clash of Kareth and Clash of Morvenn.

## Family
- Wife: Seryn Kaldreth
- Son: Bren Morr
- Closest friend: Grommak
