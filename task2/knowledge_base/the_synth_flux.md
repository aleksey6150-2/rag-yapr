# The Synth Flux

## Overview
The Synth Flux is an energy field that connects all living things in the Expanse. It is the source of power for Veylar and Dorveth alike, and understanding of the Synth Flux has shaped stellar civilization for tens of thousands of years.

## Two Sides
The Synth Flux has two aspects: the Luminar Current, associated with peace, serenity, and selflessness, and the Dorveth Current, associated with passion, anger, and hatred. The Luminar Current is practiced by the Veylar, the Dorveth Current by the Dorveth.

## Abilities
Flux users can perform feats beyond normal physical limits, including telekinesis, enhanced reflexes, mind influence, precognition, and healing. Dorveth Current practitioners can additionally produce Flux lightning and other destructive abilities.

## Sensitivity
Only certain individuals are strong enough with the Synth Flux to use it actively. Flux sensitivity is partly hereditary but can appear in unexpected lineages. The Veylar traditionally identified Flux-sensitive children early in life.

## Philosophy
The Veylar view the Synth Flux as a gift to be used in service of others, while the Dorveth view it as a power to be harnessed for personal dominion. Balance between the two remains a subject of philosophical dispute.
