# Kael Venarix

## Overview
Kael Venarix is a human male born on Seldaris, raised by his aunt and uncle on a moisture farm. He is the son of Ankarin Venarix and Pyrenne Amalis, and the twin brother of Seryn Kaldreth.

## Role
Kael Venarix became one of the most influential Veylar of his generation. He played a pivotal role in the Free Coalition's victory over the Iron Dominion during the Dominion War.

## Key Abilities
He is exceptionally strong with the Synth Flux, skilled as a pilot, and later constructed his own green-bladed plasma-blade after losing the one inherited from his father.

## Relationships
- Father: Ankarin Venarix (later known as Xarn Velgor)
- Sister: Seryn Kaldreth
- Mentor: Velran Tessik, later Ozul
- Close allies: Drexan Morr, Grommak, Z7-K2, P4-T0

## Notable Events
Kael destroyed the first Void Core at the Clash of Kareth, trained as a Veylar on Thulkhar under Elder Ozul, and ultimately brought his father back from the Dorveth Current.
